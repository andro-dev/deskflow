# FDM Group

Talent **solutions** firm: they attract people (Careers), train them (often from mixed backgrounds), and deploy **AI-enabled talent** to Clients. Closer to “academy + bench + client project” than a pure job board.

## What they do (from their public site)

* Careers path: training and placements at client businesses.
* Clients path: accelerate work with their talent, including AI-enabled delivery.
* Investors path: the firm as a portfolio, not just a desk.
* Partnerships with large tech vendors.

## Why it matters

If Tekforce ever **employs** consultants and sells them to clients (classic staff aug W-2/corp-to-corp bench), that is an FDM-shaped **Employ + Recruit** hybrid, not Robert Half’s “we introduce you.” Architecture should allow a `worker` who is on Tekforce’s bench, distinct from a candidate you submit for a client to hire.

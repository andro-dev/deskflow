# Robert Half

Specialized staffing brand: **find jobs** and **hire talent**, recruiter in the middle, practice areas on the homepage.

## What they do

* Two public doors: job seekers search/apply; employers request talent or work with a recruiter.
* Practice areas on the home page (Finance & Accounting, Technology, Marketing & Creative, Legal, Admin).
* Engagement types: contract / flexible, permanent, executive search.
* Later: client portal (track jobs, matches, interviews), salary guides, many offices.

## Do not copy

Their scale, AI marketing claims, or layout pixel-for-pixel. Copy the **motion**: two doors, specialties, recruiter desk.

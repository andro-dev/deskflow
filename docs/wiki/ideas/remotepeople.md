# RemotePeople

Two top-level product menus: **Recruit** and **Employ**. Recruit is a staffing motion. Employ is Employer of Record, Contractor of Record, global payroll, PEO — they become the legal employer.

## What they do

* **Recruit:** in-country recruiters, shortlist (they claim four vetted candidates in 72 hours), optional bundle with EOR.
* **Employ:** EOR, contractor-of-record, payroll, US PEO — compliance, contracts, pay, benefits without the client’s local entity.
* One vendor for “find someone” and “put them on payroll somewhere.”

## Ideas for Tekforce

| Idea | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information architecture: Recruit vs Employ | **watch** | Homepage can show both menus; Employ pages can be “coming soon” |
| Shortlist SLA (e.g. N candidates in X days) | later | Needs recruiter capacity, not just software |
| EOR / COR / global payroll | later | Different company, licenses, insurance |
| Contractor misclassification product | later | Legal product, not ATS |
| Bundle “we found them + we employ them” | later | Powerful for international clients; not v1 |

## Why it matters

Tekforce’s long-term story (staff aug **and** “we can employ them”) looks like this split. v1 is Recruit + ATS/CRM only. Keep Employ as a **named future module** in the architecture so we do not paint ourselves into a corner.
